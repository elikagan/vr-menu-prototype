Section II — Preflight Inspection
2-1. GENERAL.
This section contains procedures for performing preflight inspection of aircraft systems. All inspections shall be performed in the sequence listed.
⚠ WARNING Ensure all ground safety pins are installed and hydraulic pressure is verified before performing flight control checks.
2-2. HYDRAULIC SYSTEM CHECK.
a. Verify System A pressure gauge indicates between 2,800 and 3,200 PSI.
b. Verify System B pressure gauge indicates between 2,800 and 3,200 PSI.
c. Check that no amber HYD advisory warnings are illuminated on caution panel.
d. If pressure is not within limits, do not continue. Refer to T.O. 1F-16CJ-2-70GS-00-1.
2-3. FLIGHT CONTROL OPERATIONAL CHECK.
a. Move control stick full LEFT. Verify left aileron trailing edge rises.
b. Move control stick full RIGHT. Verify right aileron trailing edge rises.
c. Move control stick full FORWARD. Verify elevator deflects downward.
d. Depress left rudder pedal fully. Verify rudder deflects left.
e. Return all controls to neutral position.
CAUTION Do not exceed normal control force limits during operational check.
2-4. AVIONICS SYSTEMS VERIFICATION.
a. Verify FMS has completed initialization.
b. Verify NAV radio frequencies are set per mission briefing.
c. Verify transponder squawk code is set per ATC assignment.
d. Initiate autopilot BIT. Verify BIT completes with no faults.
2-5. ENGINE INSTRUMENT CHECK.
a. Verify oil pressure: 40-65 PSI.
b. Verify EGT is within normal idle limits.
c. Verify fuel flow matches expected idle flow rate.
