⚠ WARNING Do not perform hydraulic checks with engine running unless ground safety pins are installed in all flight control surfaces. Failure to comply may result in serious injury or death.
3-8. HYDRAULIC SYSTEM PREFLIGHT CHECK.
Perform the following steps prior to engine start.
a. Inspect hydraulic reservoir sight glass. Fluid level must indicate within the GREEN band.
b. Check System A and System B pressure gauges on the left main instrument panel.
1. System A: 2,800 to 3,200 PSI.
2. System B: 2,800 to 3,200 PSI.
CAUTION If either system indicates below 2,800 PSI, do not continue. Refer to T.O. 1F-106A-2-70, para 4-12.
c. Inspect all visible hydraulic lines and fittings for evidence of leakage (wet spots, staining, drips).
d. Verify no amber HYD caution lights are illuminated on the caution/advisory panel.
e. Cycle the speed brake control handle — OPEN then CLOSED. Verify smooth operation with no binding or hesitation.
f. Return all controls to the neutral/stowed position.
NOTE Minor seepage at fitting B-14 is acceptable provided fluid loss does not exceed 2 drops per minute. Record in AFTO Form 781A if observed.
